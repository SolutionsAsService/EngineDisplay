Every one of them does the same four things

Each cylinder repeats a cycle of four strokes over two turns of the crank. What differs is how many cylinders share the work, how they are arranged, and what lights the fuel.

1

Intake

The piston falls and draws in air (and, in a petrol engine, fuel).
2

Compression

It rises and squeezes the charge, in the diesel hard enough to make it hot enough to ignite.
3

Power

The charge burns and drives the piston down. Its first 70° make the piston crown glow.
4

Exhaust

It rises again and pushes the burnt gas out, ready to start over.
